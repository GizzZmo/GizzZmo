### Who I am

Born 1977 in Eidsvoll, Norway. I started on a Commodore 64 in 1985 and never really stopped.  
I build at the intersection of **agentic AI**, **professional audio DSP**, **privacy-first tools**, and **creative systems** — software that is both practical today and designed to grow.

> “Creativity is intelligence having fun.”

I care about local-first architecture, sources that can be checked, and tools that respect the user.  
Art, science, and technology are not separate lanes for me — they are the same road.

---

### Currently focused on

- **Omni-Grid 2.0** — local-first cyberpunk super app (bento widgets, zero telemetry)
- **Agentic systems** — multi-agent orchestration and autonomous workflows
- **Audio / DSP** — JUCE-based tools and real-time processing (DSP4Guitar and related)
- **Open knowledge** — martial arts literature, primary sources, and careful bibliography

**Open to** collaboration on agentic AI, professional audio software, privacy-first tooling, and serious open-source knowledge projects.
